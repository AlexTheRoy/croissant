🥐Budget Pace Alerts

Drag the dark button up onto your Chrome bookmarks bar (press ⌘⇧B to show it if hidden). Please don't click it on this page: dragging saves it, while clicking just runs it here, where there's no GOAL data.

Updating? Delete the old bookmark first, then drag this one in. Your saved budget edits and settings carry over.

2Use it

  1. Open your GOAL admin dashboard and make sure you're signed in.
  2. Click the Budget Pace Alerts bookmark. A panel opens in the bottom-right corner and the tab icon turns into a 🥐 so you can spot it.
  3. First time only: click Enable notifications so alerts reach you even when you're on another tab.
  4. Leave the GOAL tab open. It re-checks every 5 minutes (adjustable) and alerts you as accounts cross each threshold.

Click the bookmark again any time to reopen the panel. It only reads data. It never changes budgets, bids or anything else in GOAL.

3What the panel shows

Every account with a daily budget, sorted by how close it is to its cap. Click an agency name to open it in GOAL in a new tab. One alert per threshold per account per day; counters reset at midnight Pacific (GOAL's billing day).

Budgets are built in from your {{AS_OF_LONG}} pull. If a budget is wrong or missing, type the correct daily number in the Budget cell. It's saved on this computer and used from then on (the box turns blue). Clear the box to go back to the built-in number. Accounts marked “no budget set” won't alert until they have a number. The panel shows a reminder once the built-in budgets are two weeks old.

ⓘGood to know

  1. It only runs while the GOAL tab is open in Chrome. Close the tab and it stops.
  2. Thresholds, check frequency and the chime are all adjustable at the bottom of the panel.
  3. The first check after you click the bookmark sets a baseline: accounts already past a threshold are noted in the log, not alerted. New crossings alert from then on.
  4. If you see “GOAL session expired,” sign back into GOAL and click the bookmark again.
